Frequently asked questions

Find a quick answer about delivery, plant care, or joining Agrismart.

How fast do nursery deliveries arrive in Amman?

Most confirmed Amman orders arrive the same day or within 24 hours, depending on availability and delivery conditions. We’ll coordinate timing after your order is confirmed.

Can I get a plant health diagnosis before ordering remedies?

Yes. Use the AI Assistant to describe symptoms or share a plant photo for guidance, then contact our team if you need help choosing a treatment.

How do local nursery owners register on Agrismart?

Choose “Create Account” and select the Trader & Nursery account type to share your store details with the Agrismart team.

What is the return policy for live plants?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order details and clear photos. Our team will review plant condition and help with the next step.
